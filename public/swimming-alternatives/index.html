Swimming alternatives to Broken Beach

Broken Beach is a viewpoint, not a swimming beach, and Angel's Billabong's pool is unsafe in rough seas. For actual swimming, look to genuinely swimmable bays and confirm conditions locally.

Why not the cliff sites

Broken Beach has no verified safe descent to the water, and Angel's Billabong is a sea pool exposed to swell. Treat both as view-only in anything but calm conditions.

Where swimming is realistic

Crystal Bay, on the north-west coast, is the common sheltered swimming and snorkelling spot in this area. Always confirm the day's sea conditions with locals before entering.

Confirm before you go

Sea state changes fast. Ask about currents, waves and any local warnings, and never treat a regional model reading as a green light for water entry.

  • Crystal Bay is the main local swimming option.
  • Ask locals about conditions each day.
  • Keep children and weak swimmers close.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can I swim at Broken Beach?

No verified safe access. Treat it as a viewpoint and choose a genuinely swimmable bay instead.

Where can I swim near the west coast?

Crystal Bay is the common sheltered option. Confirm sea conditions locally before entering.

Is Angel's Billabong safe for swimming?

Only in calm seas, and even then the pool is exposed to surge. Confirm locally and avoid rough conditions.
