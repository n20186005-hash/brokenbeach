The short walk to the viewpoint

Broken Beach is experienced mainly from the cliff-top viewing area rather than a long trail. Allow about 45 to 60 minutes as a planning suggestion for arrival, orientation and photos from permitted areas.

What the path is like

Visitors reach the cliff rim and look down into the circular cove through the rock arch. The walking is short and mostly on the same headland that also serves Angel's Billabong a few minutes away by road.

How long to allow

Treat 45 to 60 minutes as a planning suggestion, not an official duration. Add travel time to and from your harbour or hotel, and confirm access and any closure on the day.

Pairs well with Angel's Billabong

Because Angel's Billabong is so close, many visitors combine the two in one stop. Keep their access rules separate, and do not assume a path between them is open or safe.

  • Park once and walk between the two viewing areas if the local route is open.
  • Carry water; shade and facilities are limited.
  • Wear sturdy footwear for uneven limestone.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is there a long hiking trail at Broken Beach?

No. The experience is a short walk to the cliff-top viewpoint. There is no verified safe descent into the cove.

How much time should I plan?

About 45 to 60 minutes for orientation and photos from permitted areas, plus your travel time to the site.

Can I walk from Broken Beach to Angel's Billabong?

They are on the same headland and close by road. Whether a walking connection is open and safe should be confirmed locally; do not assume it.
