Between Kelingking and Broken Beach

Kelingking Beach and Broken Beach are both headline Nusa Penida stops, but they sit on different parts of the island. Linking them needs a planned island drive.

Where Kelingking is

Kelingking Beach is on the island's south-west, famous for its cliff shape. From there, Broken Beach on the west coast is roughly 30 to 50 minutes by the island's rough roads.

Linking the two

Most visitors join them with a west-coast loop that also includes Angel's Billabong, Paluang Cliff and sometimes Crystal Bay. A private car with driver makes the connection far easier than multiple scooter trips.

Order and timing

A common order is Broken Beach and Angel's Billabong first, then Paluang Cliff, then Kelingking later in the day. Confirm road conditions and leave buffer time.

  • Treat 30 to 50 minutes as a road estimate, not a guarantee.
  • Do not attempt to walk between them; the distance is too far.
  • Pre-book a full-day driver to connect both.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How far is Broken Beach from Kelingking?

Roughly 30 to 50 minutes by the island's rough roads, because they are on different parts of Nusa Penida.

Can I walk between Kelingking and Broken Beach?

No, the distance is too far across rough terrain. Use a car or scooter transfer.

Should I visit both in one day?

Yes, they are usually combined in a west-coast loop with Angel's Billabong and Paluang Cliff.
