Getting here

Sampalan to Broken Beach

Some fast boats arrive at Sampalan, also called Banjar Nyuh, on Nusa Penida's north-east coast. From there, the west coast is a longer drive than from Toyapakeh.

Where Sampalan is

Sampalan, often called Banjar Nyuh, sits on the north-east of Nusa Penida. It is a valid arrival point, but it is farther from the west-coast sites than Toyapakeh.

The longer road leg

Expect roughly 45 to 70 minutes of island road from Sampalan to Broken Beach, depending on condition. If your priority is the west coast, Toyapakeh is usually the more direct harbour.

When Sampalan makes sense

Sampalan can suit travellers who also plan north or east-coast stops, or whose boat only serves that harbour. Plan the full day around the longer transfer.

  • Confirm which harbour your boat uses before booking.
  • Budget extra road time if you arrive at Sampalan.
  • Pre-book the island transfer for the whole day.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can I reach Broken Beach from Sampalan?

Yes, but it is a longer drive, roughly 45 to 70 minutes by road, because Sampalan is on the north-east coast.

Is Toyapakeh or Sampalan better for the west coast?

Toyapakeh is usually more direct for Broken Beach and the west coast. Sampalan adds road time.

Why do boats use different harbours?

Operators serve Toyapakeh or Sampalan depending on route and conditions. Confirm your arrival harbour when booking.
