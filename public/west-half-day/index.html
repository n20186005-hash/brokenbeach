West coast in a half day

If you arrive on a morning boat, a half day is enough to cover the main west-coast stops: Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong, Paluang Cliff and Crystal Bay.

A sample order

Land at Toyapakeh, then go straight to Broken Beach and Angel's Billabong on the same headland, continue to Paluang Cliff, and finish at Crystal Bay for swimming and sunset.

Timing

Allow about 45 to 60 minutes at Broken Beach, similar at Angel's Billabong, a shorter stop at Paluang Cliff, and the rest of the afternoon at Crystal Bay. Add rough-road buffer.

Make it smooth

Book a full-day driver so the loop is continuous, carry water and cash, and confirm sea conditions before any water activity at Crystal Bay.

  • Land at Toyapakeh for the shortest transfer.
  • Use one driver for the whole loop.
  • Finish at Crystal Bay for swimming and sunset.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can I see the west coast in half a day?

Yes, a morning arrival and a focused loop can cover Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong, Paluang Cliff and Crystal Bay.

What order works best?

Broken Beach and Angel's Billabong first, then Paluang Cliff, then Crystal Bay last for water and sunset.

Do I need a driver?

A full-day driver makes the rough-road loop continuous and far easier than multiple scooter trips.
