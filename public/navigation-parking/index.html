Finding Broken Beach and parking

Mobile coverage on Nusa Penida can drop, so navigation works best with an offline map saved before you sail. The map pin is a location reference, not a verified entrance.

Use offline maps

Download an offline map of Nusa Penida and mark Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong and your harbour before leaving Bali. Signal gaps are common inland.

The pin is a reference

The map pin sits in Sakti and gives a location for orientation. It is not a confirmed usable entrance or parking lot, so follow local signs and staff once you arrive.

Parking

A parking area is often mentioned near the west-coast stops, but its exact location, capacity and fee were not verified. Confirm with staff and never assume the pin marks the lot.

  • Save offline maps before the boat.
  • Share your driver's local knowledge rather than relying only on GPS.
  • Ask staff about parking location and any charge.
