Crystal Bay to Broken Beach

Crystal Bay is on Nusa Penida's north-west coast and is known as a calm swimming and snorkelling spot. It pairs naturally with the west-coast viewpoint circuit.

Where Crystal Bay is

Crystal Bay sits on the north-west coast, roughly 45 to 70 minutes by road from Broken Beach depending on conditions. It is a different kind of stop: a sheltered bay rather than a cliff arch.

Combining the two

Because both are on the west side, many visitors link Crystal Bay with Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong and Paluang Cliff in a single day, swimming at Crystal Bay and viewing the arch elsewhere.

Order and timing

A common plan puts the cliff viewpoints in the morning and Crystal Bay later for swimming and sunset, but confirm sea conditions before any water activity.

  • Treat 45 to 70 minutes as a road estimate, not a guarantee.
  • Crystal Bay is a swimming spot; Broken Beach is a viewpoint only.
  • Confirm sea conditions before entering the water anywhere.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How far is Broken Beach from Crystal Bay?

Roughly 45 to 70 minutes by road, because Crystal Bay is on the north-west coast.

Can I swim at Crystal Bay and view Broken Beach the same day?

Yes, they are often combined in a west-coast loop; Crystal Bay for swimming, Broken Beach for the viewpoint.

Is Crystal Bay safe for swimming?

It is a more sheltered bay, but always confirm the day's sea conditions locally before entering the water.
