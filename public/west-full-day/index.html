West coast full day plan

A full day lets you add Kelingking Beach to the core west-coast loop without rushing, with time for a proper lunch and swimming at Crystal Bay.

A relaxed order

Start at Broken Beach and Angel's Billabong, add Paluang Cliff, break for lunch near Kelingking, view Kelingking, then end at Crystal Bay for swimming and sunset.

Why a full day

The island's rough roads make the loop slower than the map suggests. A full day absorbs delays and gives each stop real time instead of a photo dash.

Food and buffer

Plan lunch at a warung near Kelingking or on the return road, carry water and cash, and confirm sea conditions before swimming at Crystal Bay.

  • Add Kelingking to the half-day loop.
  • Lunch near Kelingking or the harbour road.
  • Finish at Crystal Bay for water and sunset.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What does a full west-coast day include?

Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong, Paluang Cliff, Kelingking Beach and Crystal Bay, with lunch and buffer time.

Is a full day better than a half day?

Yes if you want Kelingking without rushing; rough roads make a full day calmer.

Where do I eat on this route?

Near Kelingking or on the road back toward the harbour, where more warung cluster.
