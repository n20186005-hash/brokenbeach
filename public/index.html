ORIENTATION

A cove opened through stone.

Klungkung Tourism (25 April 2022) describes Pasih Uug as a coastal hollow with a rock opening connected to the sea. We do not treat the article’s diameter and age descriptions as precise measurements. Pasih Uug is also used for Broken Beach.

An address is not an entrance

Maps on 19 October 2026 uses Sakti. A 16 February 2026 Klungkung Regency report places Broken Beach in Bunga Mekar village. These are separately attributed descriptions; the pin is not a village-boundary audit or a confirmed usable entrance.

READING THE PLACE

Three layers for understanding Pasih Uug.

Coastal arch

The tourism sources describe a rock opening and cove. The formation does not establish a permitted walking route.

Limestone context

The 2022 tourism article associates the hollow with erosion and limestone collapse. This is a source summary, not a new geological survey.

Today’s conditions

Follow open paths and local directions; an old photograph or calm-looking surface does not establish safe access.

Short answers before you step forward.

Where is Broken Beach?

Maps on 19 October 2026 lists 7F82+R4, Sakti, Nusa Penida, Klungkung, Bali 80771, pin -8.7327938, 115.4508319. A 16 February 2026 regency report uses Bunga Mekar village. These descriptions are not combined into a boundary or entrance audit.

What are the opening hours?

The Maps listing checked on 19 October 2026 supplied no hours we could verify. Do not infer 24-hour opening or permission for night access.

What is the admission price?

Current charges are unverified. Ask whether area access, parking or other services are charged, what is included and what receipt is provided.

Can visitors swim or descend into the cove?

Do not treat the cove as a swimming place or look for a descent based on old photographs. Use only permitted paths and check local restrictions.

Is it the same as Angel’s Billabong?

A coastal-pool formation separate from Broken Beach’s cove and arch. Low tide or a model forecast does not grant water-entry permission; check access and rules separately.

Is the route accessible?

Maps on 19 October 2026 marked no wheelchair-accessible entrance. This is a listing field, not an audit of every route. Ask about paths and assistance needs before travelling.

What about facilities and parking?

Availability, capacity and fees are unverified. Ask staff about your needs; do not assume the pin marks an entrance, parking or a level path.

How long should I allow?

Allow 45–60 minutes as a planning suggestion for orientation from permitted areas, not an official duration. Add travel time and check access.

Can visitors walk on the arch or use a drone?

No such permission has been verified in this guide. A photograph of someone in a location is not access permission. Follow local rules and ask staff.

Do weather and sea-level data show that water entry is safe?

No. Regional model data are not cliffside measurements, water-entry permission or a safety guarantee. Follow closures and local directions.
