Tembeling: forest, spring and beach

Tembeling is a green, forested corner of Nusa Penida with a natural pool, spring and a quiet beach. It is a different kind of visit from the west-coast cliff viewpoints.

What Tembeling is

Tembeling combines tropical forest, a freshwater natural pool and a quiet beach on the island's south-east. It is a calmer, greener contrast to the open cliff coasts.

How it differs from Broken Beach

Broken Beach is a short cliff-top arch stop; Tembeling needs more walking through forest and a longer drive, roughly 60 to 90 minutes from the west coast.

Planning the trip

Because it is far from the west coast, Tembeling suits a separate day or the east side of a two-day plan rather than a quick add-on to Broken Beach.

  • Expect a long rough drive from the west coast.
  • Wear good footwear for forest paths.
  • Treat swimming spots as unverified; confirm locally.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is Tembeling?

A forested area with a natural freshwater pool and a quiet beach on Nusa Penida's south-east.

How far is Tembeling from Broken Beach?

Roughly 60 to 90 minutes by road, because it is on the opposite side of the island.

Can I add Tembeling to a Broken Beach day?

It is far; better as a separate day or part of an east-side plan rather than a quick west-coast add-on.
