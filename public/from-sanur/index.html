Getting here

From Sanur to Broken Beach

Most visitors start on Bali's mainland at Sanur, take a fast boat to Nusa Penida, then continue by road to the west coast. Confirm boat operators, schedules and fares for your travel date.

The crossing from Sanur

Fast boats run from Sanur on Bali's south-east coast to Nusa Penida, usually arriving at Toyapakeh or Sampalan (Banjar Nyuh). The sea crossing is typically around 30 to 45 minutes, but weather and swell can change timings.

Which harbour for the west coast

Toyapakeh is the closest main harbour to Broken Beach and the west-coast sites, so it is usually the more direct choice. Sampalan is farther north-east and adds road time.

The island road leg

From Toyapakeh, the drive to Broken Beach is a rough island road of roughly 20 to 40 minutes depending on condition. Pre-arrange a driver or scooter, and confirm the meeting point before you sail.

  • Book the boat and the island transfer for the same day where possible.
  • Confirm the arrival harbour with the operator.
  • Allow buffer time for rough roads and swell.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How do I get from Sanur to Broken Beach?

Take a fast boat from Sanur to Nusa Penida, typically to Toyapakeh, then continue by car or scooter to the west coast.

Which harbour should I arrive at?

Toyapakeh is closest to Broken Beach and the west coast. Sampalan is an alternative but adds road time.

How long is the whole journey?

Plan for the boat crossing plus a 20 to 40 minute island road leg from Toyapakeh, with extra buffer for weather and rough roads.
