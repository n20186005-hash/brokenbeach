Getting here

Car with driver or scooter?

Most visitors reach Broken Beach by private car with driver or by rented scooter. The choice affects comfort, safety on rough roads and how easily you link several stops.

Private car with driver

A car with driver is the easier option for the island's rough roads, especially if you plan several west-coast stops in one day. The driver handles navigation and parking while you focus on the views.

Scooter rental

A scooter gives flexibility and lower cost, but Nusa Penida roads are rough, hilly and often dusty. Only ride if you are confident on similar terrain, wear a helmet, and avoid it in bad weather.

Other options

Shared tours and arranged transfers also exist. Whatever you choose, confirm the meeting point, destination and return plan before you sail.

  • Car with driver suits groups, families and rough-road days.
  • Scooter suits confident riders in dry conditions.
  • Confirm price, duration and what is included before paying.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is a driver worth it on Nusa Penida?

For the rough west-coast roads and multiple stops, a car with driver is usually the easier and safer choice.

Can I ride a scooter to Broken Beach?

Yes, but only if you are confident on rough, hilly roads. Wear a helmet and avoid scooters in bad weather.

What should I confirm before paying?

Agree the price, duration, pickup point, destination and return plan with the provider.
