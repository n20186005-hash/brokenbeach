Comparing the west-coast stops

The west coast packs several very different stops close together. Knowing what each offers helps you order the day and avoid backtracking on rough roads.

The four headline stops

Broken Beach is a cliff-top arch viewpoint. Angel's Billabong is a sea pool nearby. Paluang Cliff is a quiet clifftop panorama. Crystal Bay, farther north-west, is a swimming and snorkelling bay.

How they differ

Three are view-only cliff or pool stops with no verified safe water entry; Crystal Bay is the main swimming option. Distances are short between the first three but longer to Crystal Bay.

A sensible order

A common order is Broken Beach and Angel's Billabong first, then Paluang Cliff, then Crystal Bay later for swimming and sunset. Confirm sea conditions before any water activity.

  • Broken Beach + Angel's Billabong: same headland.
  • Paluang Cliff: 10 to 20 minutes further.
  • Crystal Bay: 45 to 70 minutes, best for water.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Which west-coast stop is best for photos?

Broken Beach for the arch and Paluang Cliff for wide panoramas; Angel's Billabong for the pool edge.

Which one can I swim at?

Crystal Bay is the main swimming option; the cliff and pool stops are view-only or unsafe in rough seas.

What order avoids backtracking?

Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong, Paluang Cliff, then Crystal Bay last for water and sunset.
