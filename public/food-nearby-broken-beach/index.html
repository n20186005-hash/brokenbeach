Eating near Broken Beach

Food at the west-coast cliff stops is basic and not guaranteed. Small warung or drinks stalls sometimes operate near the parking and viewpoint areas, but outlets and menus were not verified.

What may be available

You may find small stalls selling drinks, snacks or simple meals near the popular west-coast stops. Their presence, hours and menus shift with season and visitor numbers.

How to prepare

Carry your own water and any essentials you need, subject to venue rules. Bring cash, since small stalls may not take cards, and do not rely on a confirmed meal at the cliff.

Where to plan a real meal

For a proper lunch, pair the west-coast loop with a stop at a warung on the way back toward the harbour or near Kelingking, where more outlets tend to cluster.

  • Carry water for the heat.
  • Bring cash for small stalls.
  • Confirm any outlet is open before relying on it.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Are there restaurants at Broken Beach?

Not verified. Small stalls sometimes operate near the stops, but do not rely on a confirmed meal there.

Can I buy water there?

Possibly from small stalls, but carry your own to be safe and bring cash.

Where should I plan lunch?

Near the harbour on the return, or around Kelingking, where more warung tend to cluster.
