When the weather turns bad

Heavy rain or rough seas make the cliff edge at Broken Beach riskier and the roads slower. Know when to skip the viewpoint and what to do instead.

When to skip the cliff

If storms, high swell or poor visibility arrive, leave the cliff area. The model data on this site is regional, not a safety guarantee, and should never replace local judgement.

Road reality

Rain turns Nusa Penida's rough roads muddy and slippery, slowing every transfer. Build extra buffer and avoid scooters in heavy weather.

Safer alternatives

If the west coast is unsafe, consider a calmer day or shift focus to a sheltered bay like Crystal Bay once seas settle, and always confirm conditions locally.

  • Leave the cliff in storms or high swell.
  • Slow down and avoid scooters on wet roads.
  • Wait for calmer seas before any water activity.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Should I visit Broken Beach in rain?

Only in light, safe conditions. In storms or high swell, leave the cliff area and avoid scooters on wet roads.

Is the road dangerous when wet?

Rough roads become muddy and slippery; slow down and add buffer time.

What can I do instead?

Wait for calmer seas, then consider a sheltered bay like Crystal Bay, and always confirm locally.
