Crystal Bay: a swimming bay, not a viewpoint

Crystal Bay is a sheltered bay on Nusa Penida's north-west coast, known for clearer water and calmer swimming than the cliff sites. It is a different kind of stop from Broken Beach.

What Crystal Bay offers

Crystal Bay is a wide, sheltered bay where visitors often swim and snorkel. A small islet sits at the mouth, and the water is usually calmer than the open cliff coasts.

How it differs from Broken Beach

Broken Beach is a viewpoint only; Crystal Bay is a water-activity bay. They are on different coasts and roughly 45 to 70 minutes apart by road.

Linking the two

Because both are on the west side, a common plan views the cliff arches in the morning and ends at Crystal Bay for swimming and sunset. Confirm sea conditions before any water activity.

  • Crystal Bay suits swimming; Broken Beach does not.
  • Allow 45 to 70 minutes of road between them.
  • Confirm sea conditions before entering the water.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can you swim at Crystal Bay?

It is a more sheltered bay and a common swimming and snorkelling spot, but always confirm the day's sea conditions locally.

How far is Crystal Bay from Broken Beach?

Roughly 45 to 70 minutes by road, because it is on the north-west coast.

Should I visit both in one day?

Yes, they combine well in a west-coast loop: viewpoints in the morning, Crystal Bay for water later.
