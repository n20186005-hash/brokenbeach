Paluang Cliff on the west coast

Paluang Cliff is a quieter west-coast viewpoint a short drive from Broken Beach and Angel's Billabong, popular for wide cliff and sea panoramas.

What Paluang Cliff is

Paluang Cliff is an open clifftop lookout on Nusa Penida's west coast. Like Broken Beach, it is a view-only stop with no verified safe descent to the water.

How to reach it

It sits roughly 10 to 20 minutes by road from Broken Beach, depending on conditions, making it an easy add-on to the same west-coast loop.

Safety and pairing

As with any cliff edge here, stay behind barriers and keep distance from the lip in rough seas. It pairs naturally with Broken Beach and Angel's Billabong.

  • View-only; do not descend to the water.
  • About 10 to 20 minutes by road from Broken Beach.
  • Add it to a west-coast loop with Angel's Billabong.
