Getting here

Roads and driving safety

Nusa Penida's roads are noticeably rougher than mainland Bali. The drive to Broken Beach crosses uneven, hilly terrain, so plan for slow sections rather than fast miles.

What the roads are like

Expect potholes, loose gravel, steep dips and narrow passes in places. After rain, sections can become muddy or slippery, and afternoon dust is common in dry weather.

Safety tips

Drive slowly, keep a safe distance, and avoid the scooter option entirely in heavy rain or on unfamiliar rough stretches. A local driver who knows the conditions is a real advantage.

Plan with buffer

Because travel times depend on road state, treat published minute estimates as rough guides and add buffer so one delay does not ruin the day.

  • Lower your speed on rough and hilly sections.
  • Wear a helmet if on a scooter.
  • Avoid night driving where possible.
  • Keep cash for any small roadside needs.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Are the roads to Broken Beach paved?

Parts are paved but rough, with potholes, gravel and steep dips in places. Expect a slow, bumpy drive.

Is it safe to ride a scooter?

Only for confident riders in good conditions. Avoid scooters in rain or on rough unfamiliar stretches.

How long does the drive really take?

Treat estimates as rough; conditions, not distance, set the time. Add buffer for delays.
