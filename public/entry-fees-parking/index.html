Fees, parking and payment: what to confirm

Current entry fees, parking charges and payment methods at Broken Beach were not verified in our check. Use the notes below as a confirmation checklist rather than a price list.

What may be charged

Coastal viewpoints on Nusa Penida sometimes involve area access, parking or local services. Whether Broken Beach charges any of these, and what is included, should be confirmed with staff on the day.

Payment methods

Small roadside stops and parking attendants may prefer cash in Indonesian rupiah, and card or e-wallet acceptance is not guaranteed. Carry enough cash and ask for a receipt where one is offered.

Questions to ask on site

Ask whether there is an entrance or area fee, what parking costs, and what the payment options are. Keep any receipt as proof of what you paid.

  • Is there an area or entrance charge?
  • What is the parking fee, if any?
  • Which payment methods are accepted?
  • What does the charge include?

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How much is the entrance fee?

We could not verify a current fee. Ask staff on site whether an area, entrance or parking charge applies and what it covers.

Can I pay by card?

Card or e-wallet acceptance is not guaranteed at small coastal stops. Carry cash in rupiah and confirm the method before paying.

Is parking available?

A parking area is often mentioned, but its location, capacity and fee were not verified. Confirm with staff and do not assume the map pin marks the lot.
