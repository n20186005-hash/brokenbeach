Accessibility and mobility notes

The map listing we checked marked no wheelchair-accessible entrance. That is a listing field, not a full route audit, so ask about paths and assistance before you travel.

What the listing showed

The source we reviewed indicated no wheelchair-accessible entrance for Broken Beach. This tells you the formal listing, not every path on site, so treat it as a starting point for planning.

For elderly or less mobile visitors

The walking is short but on weathered limestone with uneven ground and cliff edges. Allow extra time, wear stable footwear, and ask about the condition of the viewing approach on the day.

Questions to ask locally

Before going, ask about path surface, distance from parking to the viewpoint, and whether assistance is available.

  • Is the path from parking to the rim flat or stepped?
  • How far is the viewpoint from where vehicles stop?
  • Is help available for those who need it?
