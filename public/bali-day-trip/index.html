A Bali day trip to Broken Beach?

A same-day round trip from Bali to Broken Beach is possible but tight. It works best as a focused west-coast loop rather than trying to see the whole island.

The time budget

You need a fast-boat crossing each way, roughly 30 to 45 minutes on the water, plus a 20 to 40 minute island road from Toyapakeh and the time at each stop. Early boats matter.

Keep it focused

For a day trip, concentrate on the west coast: Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong, Paluang Cliff and maybe Crystal Bay. Skip the far east and south to protect your return boat.

Reduce risk

Book the boat and a full-day driver together, confirm the last return sailing, and build buffer for swell and rough roads.

  • Take the earliest boat you can.
  • Pre-book boat plus island driver.
  • Confirm the last return sailing before setting out.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit Broken Beach from Bali in one day?

Yes, but it is tight. Focus on the west coast and take early boats both ways.

How early should I leave Bali?

As early as possible; the crossing plus island road eats most of a morning before you reach the cliff.

Should I pre-book everything?

Yes. Book the boat and a full-day driver together and confirm your last return sailing.
