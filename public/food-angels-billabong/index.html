Food & drink

Coffee and cold drinks near Angel's Billabong

Angel's Billabong and Broken Beach share the same headland, so the same basic food notes apply: small stalls may appear near the parking and viewpoint, but nothing is guaranteed.

What you may find

Near the west-coast stops, small warung sometimes sell coffee, cold drinks and simple snacks. Their hours and stock shift with the season and visitor flow.

How to prepare

Carry water and any food you need, and bring cash. Do not plan a sit-down meal at the cliff; save that for a warung closer to the harbour or Kelingking.

Pair with a real stop

If you want coffee or a proper break, combine the loop with a warung stop on the return leg toward Toyapakeh or near Kelingking.

  • Bring cash for small stalls.
  • Carry water; the coast is hot and exposed.
  • Do not rely on outlets being open.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is there coffee near Angel's Billabong?

Small stalls sometimes sell coffee and cold drinks, but hours and stock are not guaranteed.

Can I get cold drinks there?

Possibly from small stalls; carry your own water to be safe.

Where should I plan a proper break?

Near the harbour on the return, or around Kelingking, where more warung cluster.
