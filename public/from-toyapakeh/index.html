Getting here

Toyapakeh to Broken Beach

Toyapakeh is the main harbour closest to the west coast, so it is usually the most direct gateway to Broken Beach. The remaining trip is a rough island road.

The road leg

From Toyapakeh, Broken Beach is roughly 20 to 40 minutes by road depending on condition. The route crosses the island's rugged interior, so expect bumps and slow sections rather than a smooth highway.

Arranging the transfer

Most visitors pre-book a private car with driver or rent a scooter on the island. Agree the pickup point at the harbour, the destination and the return plan with the provider before you sail.

Combine with nearby stops

Because Toyapakeh feeds the whole west coast, it is easy to pair Broken Beach with Angel's Billabong, Paluang Cliff and Crystal Bay in one loop.

  • Confirm the harbour arrival with the boat operator.
  • Book the driver or scooter for the full day, not just one drop-off.
  • Carry cash for parking and small stalls.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How far is Broken Beach from Toyapakeh?

Roughly 20 to 40 minutes by road depending on conditions; it is the closest main harbour to the west coast.

Should I book a driver from the harbour?

Most visitors book a private car with driver or scooter for the day. Agree pickup, destination and return before sailing.

Can I visit other west-coast spots from here?

Yes. Toyapakeh is the natural start for a loop including Angel's Billabong, Paluang Cliff and Crystal Bay.
