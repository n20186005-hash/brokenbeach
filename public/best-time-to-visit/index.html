Choosing the right day and season

Broken Beach is an open cliff-top viewpoint, so the main variables are sea state, weather and road conditions rather than a fixed opening time. The April to October dry season generally brings calmer seas and clearer views.

Season patterns

Nusa Penida's dry season, roughly April to October, tends to have calmer seas and better visibility. The November to March wet season can bring heavier swell, rain and rougher island roads.

Time of day

Mornings often have clearer light and lighter afternoon wind on the west coast. Because this is a viewpoint, not a swimming beach, you are not tied to tide times for safety, but high swell still makes cliff edges riskier.

Suggested length

Plan 45 to 60 minutes for Broken Beach itself, and pair it with Angel's Billabong and other west-coast stops for a half-day loop. Confirm access and weather before you set out.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is the best month to visit?

The dry season from April to October usually has calmer seas and clearer views, though conditions vary year to year.

Is Broken Beach open all day?

A fixed opening time was not supplied by the map listing we checked. Treat it as an open coastal viewpoint and confirm any local access rules on the day.

How long should I spend there?

About 45 to 60 minutes for orientation and photos, before adding travel and nearby stops.
