Lunch between Broken Beach and Kelingking

Linking Broken Beach with Kelingking Beach makes a natural west-coast day, and lunch fits best at a warung near Kelingking or on the return toward the harbour.

Where outlets cluster

More food outlets tend to gather around the Kelingking area and along the road back toward Toyapakeh than at the quiet cliff stops themselves.

Timing the meal

A practical order is Broken Beach and Angel's Billabong in the morning, then Kelingking, with lunch at a warung nearby before the drive back.

What to expect

Expect simple Indonesian warung meals rather than international restaurants. Confirm opening and bring cash, since cards are not guaranteed at small places.

  • Eat near Kelingking or on the return leg.
  • Expect simple local meals.
  • Carry cash and confirm opening hours.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I eat between Broken Beach and Kelingking?

Around Kelingking or on the road back toward Toyapakeh, where more warung cluster.

Are there restaurants at the cliff stops?

Not verified; the cliff stops are view-only with at most small stalls. Plan a real meal elsewhere.

What kind of food is available?

Simple Indonesian warung meals; bring cash and confirm hours.
