Angel's Billabong next to Broken Beach

Angel's Billabong is a separate coastal-pool formation a few minutes from Broken Beach by road. It is a different place with its own access and water rules.

What it is

Angel's Billabong is a natural rock pool fed by the sea, sitting on the same western headland as Broken Beach. Unlike the arch viewpoint, the pool is a water feature where people sometimes stand at the edge.

How it differs from Broken Beach

Broken Beach is a cliff-top viewpoint over a cove; Angel's Billabong is a sea pool at a lower level. The two are close but their access and safety rules are not the same.

Safety rules

The pool is exposed to swell and can be dangerous when waves wash over the rocks. Low tide or a regional model reading does not make entry safe; check local conditions and never go in during rough seas.

  • Visit at calmer sea states, never during big swell.
  • Keep clear of the open-ocean side of the pool.
  • Confirm access and rules on the day.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is Angel's Billabong the same as Broken Beach?

No. It is a separate sea-pool formation nearby, with its own access and water rules.

Can you swim in Angel's Billabong?

People sometimes stand at the pool edge, but the sea can surge over the rocks. It is unsafe in rough conditions; confirm locally and never enter during big swell.

How far is it from Broken Beach?

A few minutes by road on the same headland; some visitors walk between them if the local route is open.
