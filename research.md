# Public source notes

Maps snapshot checked 19 October 2026: [Broken Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5mK4b222DzjCFH9A9), rating 4.6 from 8,325 reviews; location pin -8.7327938, 115.4508319; 7F82+R4, Sakti, Nusa Penida, Klungkung, Bali 80771. No hours, phone or operator website was supplied. The listing indicated no wheelchair-accessible entrance; this is not a complete route/accessibility audit or a confirmation of the exact entrance.

- [Klungkung Tourism, 25 April 2022](https://dispar.klungkungkab.go.id/broken-beach/): Pasih Uug name, coastal hollow and rock opening; relates the landform to erosion and limestone collapse. Do not turn its differing approximate dimensions or broad formation-age descriptions into precise measurements. The footer office hours belong to the tourism office, not the beach.
- [Regency report, 16 February 2026](https://protokol.klungkungkab.go.id/wamendagri-bima-arya-kunjungi-nusa-penida-dorong-percepatan-infrastruktur-dan-konsep-green-island-nusa-penida/): describes a government visit to Broken Beach in Desa Bunga Mekar, and names Crystal Bay separately. Attribute this administrative description alongside Maps’ Sakti label without resolving the boundary or entrance by assumption. Planned infrastructure is not completed visitor access.
- [Indonesia.travel Kelingking overview](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/destination/bali-nusa-tenggara/bali/kelingking-beach-a-marvelous-wonder-in-the-southwest-of-nusa-penida): distinguishes Broken Beach’s cove/arch from Angel’s Billabong’s natural pool. Do not transfer Kelingking’s descent description to Broken Beach, or infer permission to enter either water body.
- [Nusa Penida District, 14 December 2020](https://nusapenida.klungkungkab.go.id/cerita-menarik-terbentuknya-pasih-uug-broken-beach-legend/): publishes a local legend; it is not geological or event-history evidence.

Full description inspected, including warning/deletion search: no current rights warning or deletion nomination found on that page. New WebP preserves the scene with ordinary resizing/encoding, with attribution, source/licence links and modification note. Other legacy picture authors/licences could not be established and their served copies are replaced; no new author, licence or AI claim is invented for those originals.
- [Open-Meteo Marine API documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/marine-weather-api): sea_level_height_msl includes more than astronomical tide and uses mean sea level as datum; accuracy is limited at coasts and it is unsuitable for coastal navigation. The UI retains this distinction and never labels a time as safe water access. Attribution to Open-Meteo / DWD / Météo-France accompanies the model display.
